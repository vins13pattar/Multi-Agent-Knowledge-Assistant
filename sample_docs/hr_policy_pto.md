# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Document Owner:** People Operations
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Access Scope (suggested):** shared

## Overview

This policy describes how Paid Time Off (PTO) accrues, how it may be requested,
and how unused balances are handled at the end of the calendar year.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO at the following rates, based on tenure:

- **0–2 years:** 15 days per year (1.25 days per month)
- **3–5 years:** 20 days per year (1.67 days per month)
- **6+ years:** 25 days per year (2.08 days per month)

Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis relative to scheduled hours.
PTO accrual begins on an employee's first day of employment and is credited at
the end of each completed month.

## Requesting Time Off

1. Submit a PTO request through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance
   for requests of 3 days or fewer.
2. Requests longer than 5 consecutive business days require 3 weeks' notice and
   manager approval.
3. Manager approval is required for all PTO requests regardless of duration.
4. Blackout periods (end-of-quarter close, major product launches) will be
   communicated by department leads at least 30 days in advance.

## Carryover and Payout

- Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
- Any balance beyond the 5-day carryover cap is forfeited on December 31st,
  except where prohibited by local law.
- Upon termination, unused accrued PTO (up to the carryover cap) is paid out
  in the final paycheck in accordance with state law.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and is not subject to the accrual
caps described above. Employees receive 10 sick days per year, which do not
roll over.

## Exceptions

Employees on approved leave under FMLA or equivalent state programs should
contact People Operations directly, as PTO accrual and usage rules may differ
during a leave period.
