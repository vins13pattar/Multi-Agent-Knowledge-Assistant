# New Hire Onboarding Guide

**Document Owner:** People Operations
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2026
**Access Scope (suggested):** shared

## Week 1: Setup

- **Day 1:** IT provisions your laptop, email, Slack, and an `employee`-role
  account on the Knowledge Assistant platform. Complete the I-9 and benefits
  enrollment forms in the HR portal.
- **Day 2–3:** Complete mandatory compliance training (security awareness,
  code of conduct, anti-harassment). These are tracked in the HR portal and
  must be finished within the first 5 business days.
- **Day 4–5:** Meet with your manager to review your 30/60/90-day plan and
  get introduced to your onboarding buddy.

## Accounts and Access

Your default account role is `employee`, which lets you chat with the
Knowledge Assistant, upload documents with `shared` access scope, and view
your own conversation history. If your role requires `admin` access (for
approvals, restricted documents, or user management), your manager must
submit an access request per the Access Control & Credential Handling
Policy — this is not granted automatically.

## Key Systems

- **HR Portal:** PTO requests, benefits, payroll.
- **Knowledge Assistant:** Internal Q&A, document search, support ticket
  creation. Available at the URL shared in your welcome email.
- **Incident Channel:** `#incident-<date>-<slug>` in Slack, used only during
  active incidents per the Incident Response Runbook.

## First 30 Days

- Shadow at least 2 customer or cross-functional meetings relevant to your
  role.
- Complete any team-specific technical onboarding (repo access, local dev
  environment setup, architecture walkthroughs).
- Schedule 1:1s with key cross-functional partners identified by your
  manager.

## First 90 Days

By the end of your first 90 days, you should be operating independently on
your core responsibilities, have completed all compliance training, and have
a documented set of goals for the next quarter agreed upon with your
manager.

## Questions

Route general HR questions to People Operations, technical/systems access
questions to IT, and Knowledge Assistant usage questions to the #ai-platform
Slack channel or the Product FAQ document.
