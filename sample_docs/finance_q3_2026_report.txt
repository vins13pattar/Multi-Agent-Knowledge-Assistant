Q3 2026 FINANCIAL SUMMARY (INTERNAL)
Document Owner: Finance
Effective Date: October 5, 2026
Access Scope (suggested): restricted

OVERVIEW

This report summarizes company financial performance for the third quarter
of fiscal year 2026 (July 1 - September 30). Figures are unaudited and
subject to change following the year-end audit.

REVENUE

Total revenue for Q3 2026 was $18.4M, up 22% year-over-year from $15.1M in
Q3 2025 and up 6% quarter-over-quarter from $17.3M in Q2 2026.

- Subscription revenue: $14.9M (81% of total)
- Professional services revenue: $2.6M (14% of total)
- Usage-based / overage revenue: $0.9M (5% of total)

Net revenue retention (NRR) for the trailing twelve months was 118%,
driven primarily by expansion in the Enterprise tier.

EXPENSES

Total operating expenses were $16.2M, resulting in an operating margin of
12%, compared to 7% in Q2 2026.

- Cost of revenue (hosting, support): $4.1M
- Sales and marketing: $6.0M
- Research and development: $4.8M
- General and administrative: $1.3M

CASH POSITION

Cash and cash equivalents at the end of Q3 2026 were $42.7M, with a burn
rate of approximately $0.6M per month, giving a runway estimate of
approximately 71 months at current spend levels absent additional revenue
growth.

HEADCOUNT

Total headcount at quarter end was 214, up from 198 at the end of Q2 2026.
Engineering represented 38% of headcount, Sales and Marketing 26%, Customer
Success 18%, and G&A 18%.

RISKS AND WATCH ITEMS

- Customer concentration: the top 5 customers represent 24% of ARR, up from
  19% a year ago. Loss of any single top-5 account would materially affect
  quarterly results.
- Two enterprise renewals totaling $1.2M ARR are under active negotiation
  for Q4 and are not guaranteed to renew at current terms.

DISTRIBUTION

This document contains non-public financial information and is restricted
to Finance, Executive Leadership, and Board members. Do not forward outside
these groups without Finance approval.
