PRODUCT FAQ - MULTI-AGENT KNOWLEDGE ASSISTANT
Document Owner: Product Team
Effective Date: April 10, 2026
Access Scope (suggested): shared

Q: What is the Multi-Agent Knowledge Assistant?
A: It is an internal AI platform that lets employees ask natural-language
questions and get answers grounded in company documents, execute approved
business operations (like creating support tickets), and complete multi-step
tasks through a chat interface.

Q: What file types can I upload to the knowledge base?
A: PDF, Markdown (.md), and plain text (.txt) files are currently supported.
Uploading other file types will return a 400 error indicating an unsupported
extension.

Q: Who can see the documents I upload?
A: When you upload a document you choose an access scope: "shared" (visible
to all employees) or "restricted" (visible only to admins). Choose
"restricted" for anything containing financial data, security details, or
other sensitive internal information.

Q: How does the assistant decide when to ask a human for approval?
A: A Safety agent classifies the risk of any tool call the assistant wants to
make. Anything above "low" risk pauses execution and routes to the admin
approval queue before the action is carried out.

Q: What happens if the primary AI provider is down?
A: The platform automatically fails over from OpenAI to Anthropic (or vice
versa, depending on configuration) without requiring any action from the
user. You may notice a brief delay during failover.

Q: Can I ask about a document right after uploading it?
A: Yes, but allow a few seconds for ingestion (chunking + embedding) to
complete. The document list shows a status of "ingesting" until it becomes
"ingested" and is searchable.

Q: How do I report an incorrect or hallucinated answer?
A: Every response includes citations. If an answer looks wrong, check the
cited source first. If the citation doesn't support the claim, report it via
the feedback button so the Verification agent's behavior can be reviewed.

Q: Is my conversation history saved?
A: Yes. Conversations and messages are stored so you can return to them
later. Admins can see conversation metadata for support and audit purposes
but not raw message content unless explicitly investigating an incident.
